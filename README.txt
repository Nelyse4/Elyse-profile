VISITOR STATS (optional)
1. Sign up free at goatcounter.com and pick a code.
2. In each of the 4 .html files, find the commented GoatCounter line in <head>, replace YOURCODE with your code, and remove the <!-- and --> around it.
3. Share links with a tag per application, e.g. https://nelyse4.github.io/Elyse-profile/?ref=company-name, and check the Referrers/Paths in GoatCounter.
GoatCounter shows pages, countries and referrers, not names.

CONTACT FORM (optional)
1. Sign up free at formspree.io, create a form, copy its ID.
2. In Contact.html replace YOUR_FORM_ID in the form action with it. Submissions then arrive in your email.
